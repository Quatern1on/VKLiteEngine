Main coordinate system:
 - left-handed
 - x points right, y points up and z points away from viewer
 - x-axis is red, y-axis is green, z-axis is blue

NDC coordinate system:
 - right-handed
 - x points right, y points down and z points away from viewer
 - x and y range is [-1, 1], z range is [0, 1]

UV coordinate system:
 - U points right, V points down
 - U and V range is [0, 1]

Normal maps are in DirectX format
Winding order is counter-clockwise
